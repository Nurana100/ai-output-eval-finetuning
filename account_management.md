# Nimbus Notes — Account Management

**Signing up:** Create an account with email, Google, or Apple sign-in. Email verification is required within 24 hours or the account is suspended pending verification.

**Cancelling a subscription:** Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel Plan. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period; no partial refunds are given for the unused portion of a monthly plan.

**Refund policy:** New paid subscriptions are covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee from the date of first purchase. Refunds are not available for annual plans after the 14-day window, or for renewals.

**Changing plans:** You can upgrade at any time, effective immediately with a prorated charge. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

**Deleting your account:** Settings > Account > Delete Account. This is separate from cancelling a subscription — deleting the account also removes all notes per the 90-day data retention policy described in the Privacy & Security document.
