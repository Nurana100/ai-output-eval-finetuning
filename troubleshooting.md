# Nimbus Notes — Troubleshooting

**Notes not syncing:**
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Confirm you're signed in with the same account on all devices.
3. Open Settings > Sync and tap "Force sync now."
4. If the issue persists after 15 minutes, contact support with your account email and device OS version.

**Can't log in:**
- Use the "Forgot password" link on the login screen to reset your password.
- If you signed up with Google or Apple sign-in, use that same provider to log in; password reset does not apply to social logins.
- Accounts are temporarily locked after 5 failed login attempts, for 30 minutes.

**Exporting data:**
- Go to Settings > Export. You can export all notes as Markdown (.md), plain text (.txt), or PDF.
- Bulk export of the entire account as a .zip is available on Pro and Team plans only.

**App crashes on startup:**
- Update to the latest app version.
- Clear the local cache in Settings > Storage > Clear Cache (this does not delete synced notes).
