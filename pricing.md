# Nimbus Notes — Pricing

Nimbus Notes offers three plans:

- **Free** — $0/month. Up to 100 notes, 1 device sync, 7-day version history.
- **Pro** — $8/month (or $76.80/year, a 20% discount). Unlimited notes, sync across unlimited devices, 30-day version history, offline mode.
- **Team** — $15/user/month (or $144/user/year, a 20% discount). Everything in Pro, plus shared workspaces, unlimited version history, and admin controls.

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial, no credit card required to start the trial.

Taxes are calculated at checkout based on your billing address. Nimbus Notes does not currently support monthly invoicing for Team plans under 5 seats; invoicing is available for Team plans with 5+ seats.
