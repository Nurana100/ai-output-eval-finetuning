# Nimbus Notes — Features

- **Offline mode**: Available on Pro and Team plans. Notes edited offline sync automatically once you're back online.
- **Markdown support**: Full markdown editing with live preview, available on all plans.
- **Tags and search**: Full-text search and nested tags on all plans. Free plan search is limited to note titles and tags only (not full body text). Pro and Team plans include full-body search.
- **Version history**: Free = 7 days, Pro = 30 days, Team = unlimited.
- **Collaboration**: Shared workspaces and real-time co-editing are Team-only features.
- **Attachments**: Free plan allows attachments up to 5MB per note. Pro and Team allow up to 200MB per note.
- **Mobile apps**: iOS and Android apps are available on all plans. Windows and macOS desktop apps are available on all plans as well.
